## Wednesday, August 19 — Buyer guides
**Working title:** Well and Septic in the Flathead Valley: The Due Diligence Most Buyers Skip
**Angle:** Most of the valley outside city limits runs on a private well and septic — and most buyers from town (or out of state) have never bought one. The well log and what it tells you, flow and water-quality testing, septic inspections and county records, what each test costs and how long it takes, and how to write the contingency so you're not paying for surprises in October. Checklist-driven, plain English.

## Thursday, August 20 — Estate & probate sales
**Working title:** The Step-Up Basis: What Selling an Inherited Montana Home Really Costs
**Angle:** The tax rule that changes everything for heirs — and that almost nobody explains: the home's basis resets to its value at the date of death, which is why selling an inherited Flathead home often owes far less tax than families fear. What the step-up is, why a date-of-death appraisal matters even if you're not selling yet, and the records a personal representative should gather now. Compassionate, concrete, with "consult your CPA/attorney" language throughout.
