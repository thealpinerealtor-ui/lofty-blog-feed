SMS / Text Messaging Privacy Policy

Ryan Berner — Licensed Real Estate Agent, Kalispell & Whitefish, Montana (Sole Proprietor). Effective July 26, 2026.

Scope

This policy covers text (SMS) conversations with the office line of Ryan Berner. Messaging on this line is conversational customer care: replies to consumers who text or call us with real estate questions, plus appointment confirmations and callback coordination.

No sharing of mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your phone number and the content of your text conversations are used only to respond to your inquiry and coordinate service you requested. We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data with any third party for their marketing.

How you opt in

You opt in by texting our published office number, by asking for a text follow-up when you call us, or by submitting a website contact form that includes consent-to-contact language. We do not purchase contact lists and we do not send unsolicited promotional broadcasts.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies with your conversation — typically a handful of messages related to your inquiry, appointment confirmations, and scheduling. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages; opt-outs are honored immediately and permanently. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us using the information below.

Data handling

Conversation records are retained in our customer relationship management system so we can serve you, and are protected against unauthorized access. We may disclose information if required by law.
